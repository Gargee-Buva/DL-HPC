First Understand Reduction

Reduction means:

Combining results from multiple threads into one final result.

Example:

Sum of array
Maximum value
Minimum value
Why Reduction Needed?

In parallel programming:

Multiple threads work simultaneously
Shared variables can create race conditions

Example:

sum += arr[i];

If multiple threads update sum together:

Wrong output may occur
Solution → Reduction

OpenMP automatically:

Creates private copy for each thread
Performs local computation
Combines all results at end

Safe and efficient.

Theory of Reduction :-

Suppose array:

1 2 3 4 5 6

Threads:

Thread 1 → 1+2+3 = 6
Thread 2 → 4+5+6 = 15

Final reduction:

6 + 15 = 21

Why Reduction Better Than Critical?

Without reduction:

This becomes slow because:

Threads wait frequently

Reduction is optimized and faster.

Important Viva Questions
What is reduction?

Combining thread-local results into one final result.

Why reduction used?

To avoid race conditions during parallel accumulation.

What is race condition?

Multiple threads updating same variable simultaneously causing incorrect output.

Why use INT_MAX for minimum?

Ensures first element becomes minimum initially.

Why use INT_MIN for maximum?

Ensures first element becomes maximum initially.

Difference between critical and reduction?
Critical	            Reduction
Slower	                Faster
One thread at a time	Thread-local computation
More synchronization	Optimized accumulation

Most Important Viva Line :-

Reduction is an OpenMP technique used to safely and efficiently combine results from multiple parallel threads into a single final result.
